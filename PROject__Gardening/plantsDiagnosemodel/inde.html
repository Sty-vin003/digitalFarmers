Select a plant category, specific plant, and any symptoms you've observed to get a diagnosis and treatment recommendations.

Quick Tips:

  • Water plants at the base to prevent fungal diseases
  • Ensure proper spacing for good air circulation
  • Rotate crops annually to prevent soil-borne diseases
  • Remove and destroy infected plant material

Note: This tool provides general recommendations. For severe infections, consult a local agricultural expert.
